Privacy Policy

Etymora · Last updated 13 September 2026

Etymora is a word-etymology app for Android, developed by Atenso. This policy explains what the app stores, where it stores it, and how you can remove it.

The short version. Etymora works fully without an account and without an internet connection. In that mode, everything you do stays on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, a copy of your learning progress is stored in the cloud so you can restore it on another device. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your learning progress for advertising. The app does show a banner ad on two screens — see section 7. Etymora Pro is an optional purchase handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details — see section 8.

1. Using Etymora without an account

Signing in is never required — not for any feature, and not for buying Etymora Pro. Without an account, the following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Words you have viewed (your history)
  • Words you have marked as favourites
  • Words you have marked as learned, and your review progress
  • Your daily streak
  • Words you have opened with the free daily allowance, and the days on which you reached the daily limit
  • Your settings (text size, daily word difficulty, widget tablet material, notification preferences, reminder time)
  • Whether you have Etymora Pro — a local copy of what Google Play reports, see section 8
  • When the app last asked you to rate it

Uninstalling the app removes all of this from your device. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this app data in the device backup kept in your Google account and restore it when you reinstall; you control this in your device's backup settings.

2. Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. Its only purpose is to let you restore your progress if you change or reset your device.

When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app, along with an account identifier that tells us which saved data belongs to you. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service, which stores these details on your device and on Google's servers for as long as your account is connected. We never receive your Google password.

Your email address is shown to you in Settings so you can see which account is connected. We do not use it to contact you, and we do not share it.

What is stored in the cloud

While signed in, a copy of the data listed in section 1 is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under an identifier tied to your account:

DataWhy
Favourites, history, learned words, review progressSo your progress survives a device change
Daily streakSo a reinstall does not reset your streak
App settingsSo your preferences follow you
The date you first opened the appThe app introduces harder words gradually over time

Beyond the account details above, nothing else about you is stored: no contacts, no location, no content you write. The word list itself is bundled with the app and is the same for everyone.

3. Crash and performance data

Etymora uses Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes so we can fix them. A crash report contains diagnostic information about the failure — the device model, the Android version, and where in the app the error happened. It also includes a random installation identifier generated by Google.

Crash reports do not contain your name, your email address, your Google account identifier, or the words you have looked up.

4. Notifications

Notifications are scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing about your reminders is sent to a server, and we cannot see whether you open them.

If you start a free trial of Etymora Pro, a reminder that the trial is about to end is also scheduled on your device, in the same way.

5. Who else can see your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. The advertising described in section 7 is handled by Google and never receives the data in section 2.

Google acts as our service provider for the features described above (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics, Google Play Billing and Google Play's in-app review) and processes this data on our behalf under Google's own terms. Your data is encrypted in transit.

We may disclose data if we are legally required to do so.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time from inside the app, without contacting us.

If you are signed in

Open Settings → Account. You will find two options:

  • Delete cloud data — removes the copy stored in the cloud. Your Google account stays connected and the data on your device is kept.
  • Delete account and all data — removes the cloud copy, deletes your account details from Firebase Authentication, disconnects your Google account from Etymora, and clears everything stored on this device.

For your security, Google may ask you to sign in again before an account can be deleted.

If you are not signed in

Open Settings → Clear all my data to erase everything stored on your device.

Other ways

Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device. If you also want the cloud copy removed and can no longer access the app, email us at the address below and we will delete it.

Crash reports are retained by Google for a limited period and are automatically deleted; because they are not linked to your account, they cannot be retrieved or removed individually.

Purchases are not affected

Deleting your data does not cancel an Etymora Pro subscription and does not remove a purchase from Google Play. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play (see section 8), and a purchase you have made is restored the next time you open the app.

7. Advertising

Etymora shows a single banner ad at the bottom of the home and discover screens. There are no full-screen ads, and no ads appear inside a word, its meaning or its etymology.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising ID together with technical information about your device and the ad request, such as your general area derived from your IP address. This is handled by Google under its own terms; we do not receive it and cannot link it to your Etymora account. Google describes how it uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

None of the data described in section 2 — your name, email address, profile photo, favourites, history, learning progress or streak — is shared with advertisers or used to target advertising.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Etymora asks for your consent before requesting any ad, and no ad is requested until you have answered. You can change that answer at any time from Settings → Ad privacy in the app. Declining does not remove ads; it means the ads you see are not personalised.

Wherever you are, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops the ID being used to personalise ads.

8. Purchases and ratings on Google Play

Etymora Pro

Etymora Pro is optional. It is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription, either of which may begin with a free trial, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. The rest of the app remains available without paying.

All purchases are processed by Google Play. Your payment method, card details and billing address are handled by Google and are never shared with us. Google describes how it handles this information in its Privacy Policy.

To unlock Pro, the app asks Google Play, on your device, which Etymora products your Google Play account owns. Google Play returns a purchase record — the product, when it was bought, whether it is active, and a purchase token — which the app uses to turn Pro on and to confirm the purchase back to Google Play, as Google requires. This record stays on your device: it is not sent to our servers, not stored in Cloud Firestore, and not linked to your Etymora sign-in.

A purchase belongs to your Google Play account, not to an Etymora account. That is why you never need to sign in to buy Pro, and why Pro returns by itself when you reinstall the app or use another device with the same Google Play account.

Managing, cancelling and refunds

Subscriptions renew automatically at the price shown until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; a free trial cancelled before it ends is not charged. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies.

Rating the app

Etymora may occasionally show Google Play's own rating window. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play and are published as Google Play describes; the app is not told whether you rated it or what you wrote.

9. Children

Etymora is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you. The deletion tools in section 6 cover this directly; for anything else, email us and we will respond.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how Etymora handles data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.
