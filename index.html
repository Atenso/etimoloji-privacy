Privacy Policy for Etimoloji

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Etimoloji app ("the App") handles information. Etimoloji is a "Word of the Day" widget app that shows the user a new word each day along with its meaning, pronunciation, and etymology.

Signing In Is Optional

This is the most important thing to understand about how the App handles your information:

  • Signing in is optional. The App works fully without an account.
  • If you do not sign in, none of your data ever leaves your device.
  • If you do sign in with Google, a copy of your progress is stored in the cloud so you can keep it when you change or reset your phone.

What Is Stored in the Cloud

The following is stored in the cloud only if you sign in:

  • Saved words (favourites)
  • Words you have seen (history)
  • Words you have marked as unearthed, and their review progress
  • Your daily streak and best streak
  • Your app settings (font size, reminder time, notification preferences)

When you sign in with Google, we also receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. These are used only to show your account inside the App.

Where It Is Stored

Cloud data is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google. This means your data is held on Google's servers, and Google's own privacy policy also applies to that data. You can read it at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Why It Is Stored

There is a single purpose: to carry your progress between devices and to prevent it from being lost when you change your phone or reinstall the App.

Your data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties. The App does not include analytics or tracking.

Word Data

The word list used by the App is bundled inside the App itself, so an internet connection is not required to use it. If you are not signed in, information about which words you look at stays on your device.

Permissions Used

  • Notifications: if you enable reminders, the App requests permission to send you notifications. These notifications are scheduled and generated entirely on your device; no data leaves your device to produce them.
  • Internet and network state: used for Google sign-in and for syncing your progress when you are signed in, and to check whether you are online before starting the sign-in flow.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Firebase for optional sign-in and cloud sync, as described above. It does not integrate any advertising or tracking services. Pronunciation playback uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech engine, which runs locally on your device.

Deleting Your Data

You can request deletion of your data by emailing atensostudio@gmail.com. Please state which Google account you signed in with, so that the correct data can be identified and removed. Requests are processed within a reasonable period, and in any case within 30 days.

Please note:

  • Signing out of the App does not delete your cloud data. To have it deleted, please send the request described above.
  • Uninstalling the App removes the data stored on your device. If you had signed in, the copy stored in the cloud remains until you request its deletion.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
