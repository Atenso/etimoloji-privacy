Privacy Policy for Etimoloji

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Etimoloji app ("the App") handles information. Etimoloji is a "Word of the Day" widget app that shows the user a new word each day along with its meaning, pronunciation, and etymology.

Information We Collect

Etimoloji does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. The App does not require an account, does not use analytics, and does not include advertising.

All data created while using the App — such as:

  • favorited words
  • word history (previously shown words)
  • "learned" word markers and streak counts
  • app settings (font size, reminder time)

— is stored locally on your device only. This data is never sent to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the App permanently removes this data.

Permissions Used

  • Notifications: if you enable the daily reminder feature, the App requests permission to send you a local notification at your chosen time. This notification is generated entirely on your device; no data leaves your device to produce it.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Third-Party Services

Etimoloji does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Pronunciation playback uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech engine, which runs locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

Etimoloji does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because the App does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
